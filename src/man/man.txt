man - show Shulker OS manual pages

SYNOPSIS
  man PAGE
  man -k WORD
  man -l

DESCRIPTION
  Shows a manual page (paged with less when it is longer than the screen).
  `man -l` lists every page, `man -k WORD` searches their summaries.
  Pages are plain text files in /opt/shulker/man (or
  /mnt/builtin/shulker/man) and /usr/local/shulker/man for apps.
  BusyBox commands have no pages; use `COMMAND --help`.
