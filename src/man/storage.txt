storage - inventories, tanks and energy, from any mod

SYNOPSIS
  storage
  storage find WORD [--json]
  storage items [--json]
  swarm find WORD

DESCRIPTION
  OC2 reaches blocks of other mods through a bus interface on a bus cable
  next to them. Anything that holds items, fluids or energy shows up: chests,
  barrels, Sophisticated Storage, drawers, AE2 ME interfaces and storage
  buses, Refined Storage interfaces, Mekanism tanks and energy cubes, Powah
  cells, machines with inventories, and so on.

  storage        every inventory (slots used, top items), tank (fluid, fill)
                 and energy store on this computer's bus
  storage find   where an item is and how many, e.g. storage find diamond
  storage items  everything, most first
  swarm find     the same on every computer of the swarm at once, added up

  Words match item ids: diamond, iron_ingot, mekanism: (a whole mod).
  Inventories bigger than 512 slots are read only that far (shown as +).

  Give a bus interface a name with the wrench and storage uses it
  ("ore-chest" instead of "inventory 2").

WHAT WORKS WITH OTHER MODS
  Item counts, fluid levels and stored energy work for every mod. Functions
  of a specific mod do not: OC2 has no driver that asks AE2 to craft, or
  changes a machine's settings. An ME interface shows the items in its own
  slots, not the whole ME network; a storage bus or interface set up to
  expose items is what you read.

SENSORS
  The monitor reads fluid.N (tank percent) and storage.N (slots used,
  percent, once a minute) for alarm rules, e.g.
      monitor add tank-low fluid.1 '<' 10 redstone:up
